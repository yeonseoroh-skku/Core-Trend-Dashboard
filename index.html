Core Trend

The "Core" trend (or “-core” aesthetic) refers to hyper-specific subcultures or micro-trends on TikTok and Instagram that define a distinct visual vibe or lifestyle.

Social Media Aesthetics (The "-core" Trend)

Originating largely on platforms like TikTok and Tumblr, the suffix "-core" is slapped onto any word to describe a specific vibe, aesthetic, or visual identity.

How it's used: It creates hyper-specific internet subcultures. For example, "lovecore" features hearts and pink motifs, while "royalcore" pulls inspiration from historical European monarchies.

The "Corecore" trend: This is an artistic, somewhat surreal editing style on TikTok that mashes together unrelated audio and video clips to evoke heavy, emotional, or nostalgic feelings about modern life.

Gorpcore

Gorpcore is a fashion aesthetic that takes high-performance outdoor, hiking, and camping gear and styles it for everyday streetwear. The name comes from "gorp," slang for trail mix (Good Ol' Raisins and Peanuts), mixed with "normcore". It celebrates utilitarian, weather-resistant clothing designed to be practical, comfortable, and versatile in urban environments.

Why People Love It

  • Climate Readiness: Frequent freak weather has made water-resistant and windproof gear everyday city wear.
  • Comfort and Utility: Rejecting constricting clothing, Gen Z gravitates toward brands that allow easy movement and possess practical elements.
  • Eco-Consciousness: Many gorpcore labels utilize recycled materials and offer repair programs, which heavily appeal to environmentally aware youth.
Arc'teryx

The brand's XT-6 trail running shoe became an instant, global streetwear icon.

Salomon

Known for minimalist tech.

The North Face & Patagonia

Historic outdoor staples that have been heavily absorbed into youth closets.

Uniqlo

Provides highly accessible, budget-friendly entry points for gorpcore vests and fleeces.

Hardshell & Rain Jackets

Waterproof, breathable shells from technical specialists like Arc'teryx or durable classics like The North Face

Fleece Pullovers & Cardigans

Cozy layering pieces. Brands like Patagonia are the gold standard for retro and modern fleeces.

Utility & Cargo Pants

Wide-leg, durable bottoms equipped with multiple pockets. Labels like Gramicci offer iconic climbing pants with built-in webbed belts.

Trail Shoes & Hiking Boots

Chunky, rugged footwear designed for the trail but styled in the city. Look for Salomon for aesthetic trail runners or HOKA for highly cushioned options.

Accessories

Ripstop crossbody slings, rugged bucket hats, and thick, chunky knit beanies.

Balletcore

Balletcore is a viral fashion aesthetic and lifestyle trend that draws inspiration from the delicate, graceful world of classical ballet. It blends the functional beauty of dancewear—like leotards, wrap tops, and tulle skirts—with everyday elevated athleisure and streetwear.

Why It’s Trending

The trend gained massive traction on social media (such as TikTok and Instagram) as a natural evolution of comfort-focused athleisure. It offers a way to look effortlessly polished, romantic, and hyper-feminine while remaining comfortable enough for daily wear.

Miu Miu

Renowned for bringing ballet flats, wrap skirts, and delicate knitwear to the luxury high-fashion runway.

Bloch

An authentic dancewear authority that offers an everyday Ballet Core Collection of wrap tops and soft knitwear.

Shushu Tong

A designer brand specializing in romantic, structured tulle, and feminine silhouettes.

Repetto

Founded in Paris in 1947, they are the gold standard for authentic ballet footwear and apparel.

Color Palette

Dominated by soft, romantic hues like pale pinks, creams, whites, nudes, and soft grays. Hints of black or wine tones are often added for contrast.

Fabrics & Textures

Flowing, ethereal materials like tulle, chiffon, and sheer fabrics mixed with sleek satin, breathable jersey, and cozy knits.

Fitted Basics

Bodysuits and camisoles that act as base layers, emulating traditional leotards.

Layering

Wrap tops, shrugs, and oversized cardigans worn over fitted tanks to accentuate the waist.

Footwear

Delicate ballet flats (often with satin finishes or ankle ribbons), Mary Janes, or pointe shoes.

Accessories

Leg warmers, knit shrugs, hair ribbons, and bows.

Hair & Makeup

Sleek, slicked-back buns, soft rosy makeup, and dewy complexions.

GeekChic

Geek chic is a fashion and lifestyle trend that recontextualizes traditionally "nerdy" or "uncool" clothing into trendy, stylish outfits. It celebrates a scholastic, intellectual, or pop-culture aesthetic—blending vintage scholastic vibes with subtle nods to gaming, or comic fandoms.

Why It Matters

Geek chic represents a cultural shift. It moved geeky and scholastic subcultures from being viewed as socially awkward to being celebrated as confident, intellectual, and highly fashionable.

Thom Browne

Known for heavily preppy, academically inclined silhouettes, pleated skirts, and tailored blazers that capture a fantastical school uniform aesthetic.

LEWKIN

A rapidly popular K-fashion brand offering distinctively smart, "luxe-librarian" styled clothing like vests, pleated skirts, and oversized glasses.

Warby Parker

The go-to destination for stylish, intellectual, and vintage-inspired frames that anchor any geek-chic outfit.

Statement Eyewear

The ultimate staple. Oversized, thick black horn-rimmed frames, tortoiseshell patterns, or wire-rimmed glasses are central.

Vintage Layering

Pieces reminiscent of a cozy, scholarly wardrobe. Think cable-knit sweaters, sweater vests, argyle prints, and oversized, worn-in cardigans.

Preppy Academia

Tailored, scholastic silhouettes such as high-waisted trousers, pleated plaid skirts, button-down shirts, and blazers.

Quirky Footwear

Practical, sturdy shoes are essential. Oxfords, loafers, and classic worn-in canvas sneakers complete the look.

Subtle References

Unlike full cosplay, geek chic integrates subtle nods to pop culture—like a vintage graphic tee, a subtle sci-fi print, or retro pixel patterns—into an otherwise everyday, curated wardrobe.

Smart Accessories

Practical, bookish additions like structured leather satchels, messenger bags, or even an occasional bow tie or pair of suspenders.

Barbiecore

Barbiecore is a hyper-feminine aesthetic and fashion trend that pays homage to the iconic Mattel doll. Driven by nostalgic energy, it features bold, monochromatic hot pink hues, playful textures, glittery accessories, and a distinctly unapologetic approach to maximalist glamour.

Why It’s Trending

Barbiecore is trending because it perfectly combines early 2000s nostalgia, dopamine dressing, and a modern celebration of hyper-femininity. The movement blends bright pink aesthetics, playful fashion, and self-expression into a cultural phenomenon.

NYX Professional Makeup

Offers highly sought-after, limited-edition eyeshadow palettes, jumbo pencils, and flip-phone mirrors.

OPI

Released a signature lacquer collection featuring a variety of bold pinks, blues, and glitter shades.

Aldo

Known for their accessible footwear and handbag capsule collections directly inspired by the Barbie universe.

Signature Color Palette

Supporting Shades: Complemented by bubblegum pink, pastel hues, lavender, and bold pops of neon.

Hyper-Feminine Fashion

Y2K & Retro Elements: Early-2000s McBling influences, such as velour tracksuits, butterfly clips, and platform shoes.
Iconic Silhouettes: Bodycon dresses, micro-miniskirts, crop tops, and dramatic, voluminous skirts that mimic a doll's proportions.

Bold Beauty & Styling

Flawless Makeup: Glowing skin with vibrant eyeshadows, colorful cat-eye liner, and glossy, bright lipstick.

Maximalist Decor & Design

Dreamhouse Vibe: Bright pink walls, velvet seating, and retro-inspired appliances.
Glamorous Details: Fluffy throw pillows, vanity mirrors, see-through vases, and gold or metallic accents for a touch of Art Deco luxury.

Cowboycore

A dominant fashion and lifestyle aesthetic that elevates traditional Western wear—like cowboy boots, Stetson hats, fringe jackets, and bolo ties—with modern, everyday styling.

Why It’s Trending

Cowboycore is trending because of high-profile pop culture endorsements, a desire for authenticity, and its versatility.

Tecovas

Known for modern, direct-to-consumer craftsmanship that eliminates luxury markups.

Ariat

Blends traditional Western silhouettes with athletic footwear technology for supreme everyday comfort.

Wrangler

The defining name in Western denim, famous for its durable "cowboy cut" jeans and snap shirts.

Lee

A heritage denim brand that frequently collaborates on trendy, accessible cowboycore capsules.

Footwear

Classic leather or suede cowboy boots are the ultimate staple. They range from neutral tones like tan and black to statement-making oxblood or detailed embroidery.

Headwear & Accessories

Wide-brimmed cowboy hats made from natural materials, heavy metal-buckled belts, bolo ties, and bandanas are used to anchor the Western vibe.

Fabrics & Textures

Heavy-duty denim (especially in double-denim "Canadian tuxedo" looks), rugged leather, distressed suede, and flowing textures like lace or fringed elements are prominent.

Silhouettes & Tailoring

Vintage-style chore jackets, western button-down shirts with contrasting yokes, blousier blouses, and skirts—often featuring ruffles or fringe—provide a blend of structure and movement.

Earthy Color Palettes

The aesthetic relies on colors found in the American West, including denim blues, dusty rust, warm ochre, sage green, deep browns, and sunset reds.

Royalcore

Royalcore is an internet aesthetic that romanticizes the opulence, elegance, and lifestyle of historical European royalty. Driven by social media and period dramas like Bridgerton, it emphasizes escapism, grandeur, and classic romance.

Why It’s Trending

Royalcore is trending because it offers a highly romanticized form of escapism, heavily boosted by historical fiction on TV and social media. It allows people to participate in flamboyant, hyper-feminine fashion and maximalist interior design, embodying an elegant, majestic lifestyle.

Selkie

Famous for their signature pastel puff dresses and playful printed organza designs.

Teuta Matoshi

Renowned for exquisite, custom-tailored formal gowns, floral bodices, and elaborate tulle skirts.

Lirika Matoshi

Known for romantic, heavily embellished, and whimsical designs that merge fantasy with modern royal aesthetics.

Regal Fashion & Silhouettes

The fashion prioritizes extravagance over strict historical accuracy. Key Garments are corsets, ball gowns, A-line skirts, and puff-sleeved dresses.

Fabrics

Luxurious and heavy materials like velvet, silk, brocade, organza, and lace.

Accessories

Tiaras, long opera gloves, pearls, and ornate brooches.

Maximalist Décor & Interiors

Furniture: Four-poster beds, velvet or throne-style armchairs, and heavily carved, dark wood pieces.
Accents: Gilded (gold) picture frames, crystal chandeliers, ornate mirrors, and candelabras.

Art & Culture

Media Inspirations: Driven by shows like Bridgerton (Regencycore) and Reign, as well as Jane Austen novels.
Audio: Classical music, symphonies, or instrumental covers of modern pop songs.
